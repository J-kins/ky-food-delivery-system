## 02. Logo System

### Primary Logo
The **KY Food Delivery System** primary logo consists of the wordmark **"KY"** in a bold, custom-styled treatment, paired with the full brand name set in Poppins SemiBold beneath it. The logo is always rendered in Forest Green on light backgrounds, or Off-White on dark/coloured backgrounds.

### Secondary Logo
The secondary logo is a condensed version of the primary — the **"KY"** wordmark alone with the descriptor "Food Delivery" in a smaller weight, suitable for horizontal lockups, app navigation bars, and co-branding scenarios.

### Icon / Mark
The standalone **"KY"** monogram mark — a bold, geometric abbreviation — serves as the icon/favicon, app icon, embossed stamp, and watermark. It must never be used without the wordmark in primary brand applications (e.g. storefronts, hero banners), but may appear solo in small-scale or repeated pattern contexts.

---

### Clear Space

Clear space around the logo must always equal the height of the accent element within the logo letterforms (referred to as **"X"**). No other graphic elements, text, or imagery may intrude within this protected zone.

```
     X
  ┌────────────────────┐
X │                    │ X
  │    KY FOOD         │
  │    DELIVERY        │
  └────────────────────┘
     X

X = Height of the accent stroke on the "K"
```

### Minimum Size

| Medium | Minimum Size |
|---|---|
| Print | 25 mm wide |
| Digital | 70 px wide |

Do not reproduce the primary logo smaller than these thresholds. Use the Icon Mark instead for smaller applications.

## 05. Visual Language

### Brand Personality Pillars

Our visual language expresses five core personality traits across every touchpoint:

- **Bold** — High contrast, decisive shapes, strong typographic hierarchy.
- **Vibrant** — Saturated colour, energetic composition, appetite-driven imagery.
- **Friendly** — Rounded forms, warm tones, approachable layouts.
- **Modern** — Clean geometry, generous whitespace, contemporary type.
- **Dynamic** — Diagonal energy, overlapping shapes, layered graphic elements.

---

### Patterns & Graphic Elements

The KY Food Delivery graphic system uses a library of geometric and organic shapes drawn from the brand colour palette:

- **Cross / X forms** in Tomato Red and Forest Green — represent intersection, connection, and speed.
- **Concentric wave arcs** in Mustard Yellow — suggest warmth, energy, and delivery in motion.
- **Diagonal stripe fills** in Sunset Orange — add tactile texture and brand energy.
- **Leaf / teardrop crescents** in Forest Green — a nod to freshness and ingredients.
- **Circular badges** in Deep Brown — used for stamps, seals, and promotional roundels.
- **Sunburst radiating lines** in Mustard Yellow — suggest speed, energy, and excitement.

Patterns are used across packaging, apparel linings, social media backgrounds, and app illustration elements. They are never applied over food photography.

---

### Shapes

The KY Food Delivery shape library consists of:

- **Rounded Rectangle / Square** — Forest Green. The primary container shape for UI cards, tags, badges.
- **Solid Rectangle** — Tomato Red. Used for CTA buttons, label backgrounds, promotional banners.
- **Half Circle / Dome** — Mustard Yellow. A brand signature shape used in corners, dividers, and decorative crops.
- **Organic Wave Line** — Deep Brown. A loose, flowing rule that adds personality to section dividers and underlines.

## 06. Photography Style

### Guiding Principles

KY Food Delivery photography is **warm, vibrant, and appetite-driven**. Every image should make the viewer crave the food and trust the experience.

- Focus on **real food** — never overly staged or artificial.
- Use **bold colours** and **strong, directional lighting** — avoid flat, shadowless studio shots.
- Capture **authentic moments** — hands reaching for food, steam rising, sauce dripping.
- Favour **close-up detail shots** — texture, ingredients, and freshness indicators.
- Always prioritise **high contrast** — food should pop against the background.

---

### Photography Checklist

| Criterion | Standard |
|---|---|
| ✅ High Contrast | Food and key elements stand out clearly |
| ✅ Warm Tones | Golden, amber, and terracotta colour grading |
| ✅ Close-Up Details | Macro or tight crops showing food texture |
| ✅ Fresh Ingredients | Visible freshness — herbs, vegetables, sauces |
| ✅ Real Moments | Candid, lifestyle-driven, not overly posed |
| ✅ Bold Composition | Rule of thirds, strong leading lines |

---

### Photo Treatment

- **Colour grading:** Warm shadows, lifted highlights, boosted saturation in the orange/red/yellow range.
- **Cropping:** Dynamic and asymmetric — avoid perfectly centred, static compositions.
- **Overlays:** A subtle Forest Green or Tomato Red colour wash at 10–20% opacity may be applied for UI screens and promotional graphics.
- **Never** apply extreme filters, heavy vignettes, or desaturation to food imagery.
- **Never** use stock photos that appear generic, artificial, or inconsistent with the brand's warmth.

## 08. Do's & Don'ts

### ✅ DO

- **Use the correct logo** — always use the approved primary, secondary, or icon mark files. Never recreate the logo from scratch.
- **Maintain clear space** — always respect the X-height clear zone around the logo.
- **Use approved colours** — only use the seven palette colours. Never introduce new colours.
- **Ensure high contrast** — always verify that text and logos meet WCAG AA contrast standards (minimum 4.5:1 ratio for body text).
- **Use the correct typefaces** — Poppins for all standard text, Baloo 2 for accents and display only.
- **Use brand photography** — only use imagery that meets the photography style guidelines above.

---

### ❌ DON'T

- **Don't distort the logo** — never stretch, compress, skew, or alter the proportions of the logo in any direction.
- **Don't change logo colours** — never apply unapproved colours, gradients, or tints to the logo.
- **Don't rotate the logo** — the logo must always appear at 0° horizontal. No diagonal or vertical orientations.
- **Don't add effects** — no drop shadows, glows, outlines, bevels, emboss effects, or reflections on the logo.
- **Don't place the logo on busy backgrounds** — always ensure sufficient contrast between the logo and its background.
- **Don't mix typefaces** — never combine Poppins or Baloo 2 with third-party decorative or system fonts in brand materials.
- **Don't use off-brand colours** — never use pure black (`#000000`), pure white (`#FFFFFF`), or any colour outside the approved palette without explicit brand team sign-off.
- **Don't reproduce the logo below minimum size** — use the Icon Mark instead.

## 09. Tone of Voice

### Our Voice: **WARM. BOLD. CONFIDENT. REAL.**

> We speak like a friend who knows great food. Our tone is upbeat, honest, and full of flavour.

---

### Voice Principles

- ✅ **We're warm and welcoming** — We greet every customer like they matter (because they do). Our language is friendly, human, and never robotic.
- ✅ **We're bold and expressive** — We don't whisper. We say what we mean with energy, personality, and a bit of swagger.
- ✅ **We're real and honest** — No corporate fluff. No empty promises. We speak plainly and deliver on what we say.
- ✅ **We're confident and consistent** — Our voice is the same whether it's a push notification, an Instagram caption, a packaging label, or a customer service response.

---

### Tone in Practice

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| App notifications | Warm, punchy | "Your order is on its way! 🍔 ETA: 18 mins." |
| Marketing headlines | Bold, appetite-driven | "Every craving. Delivered." |
| Error messages | Honest, reassuring | "Something went wrong. We're on it — try again in a moment." |
| Social media captions | Playful, expressive | "Monday just got a whole lot tastier. 🔥" |
| Customer support | Empathetic, direct | "We're sorry to hear that. Let's fix it right now." |
| Packaging copy | Warm, proud | "Made with care. Delivered with speed. That's the KY way." |
