## Design Principles Applied

1. **Mobile-First Approach:** Designs optimized for 375px first, then enhanced for tablet and desktop
2. **Component Reusability:** Cards, buttons, inputs designed as reusable patterns
3. **Consistency:** Color, typography, spacing, and layout follow design system rigidly
4. **Accessibility:** WCAG AA compliance throughout with focus states and semantic structure
5. **Professional Polish:** Rounded corners, proper shadows, micro-interactions planned
6. **Brand Alignment:** Every element reflects KY Food Delivery's bold, warm, confident identity
